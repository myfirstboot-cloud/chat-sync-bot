Privacy Policy

These applications (Chat-synce-boot, YouTube-Email-Bot, and Drive-Copier-Extension) are personal automation tools created for internal and individual use.

Data Usage

Data accessed via Google APIs (Google Chat, YouTube Data API, Gmail, and Google Drive API) is processed strictly in memory during execution to deliver notifications, synchronize authorized spaces, and copy requested files.

Specifically for the Drive-Copier-Extension: the extension requires authorization to copy files on your behalf. It does not read, download, or alter any existing files in your Google Drive, nor does it retain any personal information.

Data Sharing

No user data is stored in external databases, collected for analytics, sold, or shared with third parties.
